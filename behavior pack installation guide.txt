Conductive Sea Lantern: Version 1.0.3

The behavior pack and resource pack preserve the original Sea Lantern sounds and existing block properties.

Installation:
Close Minecraft and back up your world. Extract the archive, then open each of the two .mcpack files separately to import them into Minecraft.

Open the world settings and make sure that both the behavior pack and resource pack are enabled and updated to version 1.0.3.

If you need to repair an older world, use the provided world conversion tool.

Gameplay Balance:
To maintain gameplay balance, the legacy Sea Lantern has no crafting recipe in Survival Mode. However, mining an existing legacy Sea Lantern will drop the block itself.

The block is also available in the Creative Mode inventory.

Block ID: railfix:conductive_sea_lantern
